## MediaPipe Image Segmenter

The live hair-colour studio uses `@mediapipe/tasks-vision` and Google's Selfie Multiclass
Segmentation model. The model classifies pixels as background, hair, body, face, clothes,
or other and is used locally in the browser.

- Project: https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/image_segmenter
- Model: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_segmenter/selfie_multiclass_256x256/float32/latest/selfie_multiclass_256x256.tflite
